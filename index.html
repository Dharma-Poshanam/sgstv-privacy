Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

SGS TV has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. Your viewing progress stays on your television. Our servers see the web requests the app makes, including your IP address, and keep them in short-lived logs. Videos are hosted and played by YouTube, which is governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

Who we are

SGS TV is the television app of Avadhoota Datta Peetham, Mysuru, India (“the Peetham”, “we”). The Peetham is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy covers:

  • the SGS TV app for Android TV and Google TV;
  • the SGS TV app for LG webOS televisions;
  • the player page at play.sgstv.live, which both apps use to play videos and which can also be opened in a web browser;
  • the catalogue service at api.sgstv.live that the apps and the player page read from.

Contact: dev@dharmaposhanam.in

What we do not collect

SGS TV does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, payment details, contacts, location, microphone or camera. It does not create an account or a device identifier, does not read the advertising ID, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. We do not sell personal data.

What stays on your television

The app keeps two things in its own storage on the television:

Your viewing progress
The series you last opened, the last video you started in each series, the videos you have finished, and the point (in seconds) where you stopped a video part-way through. This is how the app resumes your series at the next video. Up to 5,000 finished and 5,000 resume entries are kept; the oldest are removed first. This record is never sent to us or to anyone else (but see what our servers receive), and on Android it is excluded from device backups.
A copy of the catalogue
The list of series and videos (titles, video IDs and thumbnail addresses, which come from YouTube), so the app can start without waiting for the network. The copy carries an expiry date set by our server, at most 30 days after the data was fetched from YouTube; the app stops using it and deletes it once that date passes.

Video thumbnails are held in memory only while the app is running and are not saved. No video or audio is ever stored by SGS TV.

To delete this data on Android TV or Google TV, open Settings, then Apps, then SGS TV, and choose Clear data or Uninstall. On LG webOS, uninstall the app.

What our servers receive

The app makes three kinds of request to api.sgstv.live:

  • the catalogue of series and videos;
  • whether the Peetham is broadcasting live, every minute while the Home screen is open (the player page, opened on its own in a browser, asks every five minutes);
  • further videos of a long series, as you reach the end of what has been loaded. This request names the video the app has reached (for example ?from= followed by a YouTube video ID).

None of these requests carries an account, a name or a device identifier. Like every request on the internet, though, each one arrives with your IP address, the time, the address requested and the technical headers your television sends (such as its software version). Because of the third kind of request, a log entry can show, for an IP address, roughly how far through a series someone has watched.

Who handles these requests

  • Cloudflare receives every request to api.sgstv.live and play.sgstv.live. Request logging is switched on for the api.sgstv.live proxy. Cloudflare keeps those logs for a limited number of days set by the account’s plan; we do not export them or keep them anywhere else.
  • Google Cloud runs the catalogue service in Mumbai, India (Cloud Run, region asia-south1). Cloud Run records a log entry for each request. These go to Cloud Logging’s default log bucket, which keeps them for 30 days; we have not configured a longer period.

Both act as our service providers. We use these logs only to keep the service running and secure and to diagnose faults. We do not combine them with other data, build profiles from them, or try to identify the people behind them.

YouTube

The videos in SGS TV are the Peetham’s own, published on YouTube. SGS TV uses YouTube API Services. Videos are played with YouTube’s embedded player inside the app, and thumbnails are loaded directly from YouTube’s image servers. When that happens your television connects to YouTube and Google directly: they receive your IP address and information about what is watched, and they may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies, under Google’s own policies. If a video cannot play inside SGS TV, the app opens it in the YouTube app on your television, where YouTube’s own terms and settings apply. We do not receive any of the data YouTube collects.

By using SGS TV you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Our server reads public information about the Peetham’s own channels from the YouTube Data API (titles, video IDs, thumbnails and whether a channel is live). It never accesses your Google account, and SGS TV never asks you to sign in to Google. If you have ever granted any app access to your Google account, you can review and revoke that access on Google’s security settings page.

Cookies

SGS TV and play.sgstv.live set no cookies of their own and store nothing in your browser. YouTube’s embedded player may, as described above.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Apart from the service providers named above, we disclose information only if the law requires us to.

Children

SGS TV carries spiritual discourses, devotional music and live broadcasts from the Peetham for a general audience. It is not designed for children, and it collects no personal information from anyone beyond what is described here.

Your rights

You can delete everything SGS TV keeps on your television at any time, as described above. You may also contact us to ask about, correct or erase personal data, or to raise a grievance, under the law that applies to you, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Our logs are not linked to names or accounts, so to find a request we would need the IP address and the approximate time.

Security

Every connection the app makes uses HTTPS; unencrypted connections are switched off.

Changes to this policy

If what SGS TV does with data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the apps.
